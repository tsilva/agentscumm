Prerequisite testing. At each obstacle, write a short hypothesis about the next state you need and the visible prerequisites that could produce it. Rank those prerequisites by current evidence and progress unlocked per action. Choose the cheapest grounded interaction that can confirm or rule out the leading hypothesis. Use ordinary observations and history to recover feedback before spending another input. Rebuild the list when the room or inventory changes. Keep a short unresolved-prerequisite list when visiting another area, and revisit a possibility when new evidence changes its requirements.
